# ROADMAP

## L0 - Propositional Validity

- propositions
- negation
- conjunction
- disjunction
- implication
- biconditional
- truth tables
- basic validity

## L1 - Typed Claims

- OBSERVED_FACT
- SOURCE_ASSERTION
- DERIVED_FACT
- ASSUMPTION
- HYPOTHESIS
- INTERPRETATION
- UNKNOWN
- ACCESS_CONSTRAINED

## L2 - Evidence Versus Validity

Distinguish:
- valid inference with unsupported premises
- supported premises with invalid inference
- soundness

## L3 - Contradiction

Detect incompatible claims while preserving competing claims rather than arbitrarily
selecting one.

## L4 - Temporal Reasoning

Represent:
- event time
- observation period
- retrieval time
- publication time

## L5 - Defeasible Reasoning

Support ordinary rules that may be defeated by stronger or contrary evidence.

## L6 - Probability and Uncertainty

Add probabilistic reasoning without destroying the typed logical core.
