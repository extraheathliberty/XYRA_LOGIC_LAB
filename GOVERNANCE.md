# GOVERNANCE

## Authority

XYRA_LOGIC_LAB operates under XYRA constitutional and Central Control governance.

Human Committee authority remains final.

## Separation of Responsibilities

### LLM / AIR-001
May propose:
- reasoning
- hypotheses
- candidate premises
- tool plans

### Logic Backbone
May evaluate:
- formal structure
- validity
- contradiction
- derivability
- reasoning type

### Evidence Systems
Evaluate whether premises are supported by evidence.

### Applications / Coach
May explain governed results.

### Human
Decides.

## Prohibited Conflation

The Logic Lab must not treat logical validity as factual truth.

The Logic Lab must not silently convert:
- assumptions into observations
- hypotheses into facts
- unknown states into negative facts
- access constraints into evidence of deception

## Domain Independence

The Logic Lab is general-purpose infrastructure.

M5V and the Toronto Economic Heat Map may be used as benchmark environments but do not
own or define the Logic Lab.

## Change Control

Material capability expansion beyond an authorized phase requires an explicit governed
node or Committee authorization.
