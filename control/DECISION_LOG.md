# DECISION LOG

## D-001 - Canonical Project Name

Decision: XYRA_LOGIC_LAB

The historical name XYRA_Logic_Machine is retained as provenance, not as the canonical
institutional repository name.

## D-002 - Clean-Room Reboot

The project may proceed despite the historical source being unrecovered.

Legacy recovery remains a deferred obligation.

## D-003 - Validity / Evidence Separation

Formal validity and evidential support are architecturally separate.

The Logic Lab may determine that a conclusion follows from premises without asserting
that those premises are factually true.

## D-004 - Domain Independence

XYRA_LOGIC_LAB is general-purpose.

M5V is an applied benchmark, not the owner of the system.
